Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy describes what data the NK101 Nepal website collects from visitors, why it is used, how long it is kept and what choices visitors have. It covers this website only; the account you open on the NK101 platform is governed by the privacy terms shown inside the platform.

  • Website only
  • Minimal data
  • Cookie choices
  • Contact form
Scope Data We Collect Cookies and Analytics Contact Form Links to the Platform and Other Sites Retention and Security Your Choices Changes to This Policy

Scope

The policy applies to pages served on this domain in Nepali and English. When you follow a Login or Register button, you leave this website and enter the NK101 platform, which processes account, payment and verification data under its own privacy terms. Please read those terms inside the platform before opening an account.

Data We Collect

This website has no user accounts and no payment functions, so the data it handles is limited.

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Technical dataIP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring pageServing the pages, measuring traffic, detecting abuse

We do not ask for identity documents, payment details or account passwords, and we will never request them by email or chat.

Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small files stored by the browser. This website may use a cookie to remember the interface language and an analytics service to count visits and see which pages are read. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify a person. Browsers allow cookies to be blocked or deleted in their settings; the site continues to work without them, although the language may need to be reselected.

Contact Form

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to the site team and stored for as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the exchange. The details are not added to mailing lists and are not shared with third parties except the hosting provider that delivers the form.

Links to the Platform and Other Sites

Buttons and links lead to the NK101 platform and to its official channels on social networks. Those services have their own privacy practices. We recommend reading them, in particular the platform's terms on account verification and payment data.

Retention and Security

Technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and then deleted or anonymised. Contact form messages are kept while the matter is open and for a reasonable time afterwards. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with providers that apply industry security standards.

Your Choices

Visitors can ask what data we hold about them, request a correction or ask for messages to be deleted. Requests are sent through the contact form on the About Us page and answered within a reasonable time. Cookie preferences are managed in the browser.

Changes to This Policy

The policy may be updated when the website changes. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision, and continued use of the site after an update means the current version applies.
