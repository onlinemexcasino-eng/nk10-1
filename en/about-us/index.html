What We Do

We explain the NK101 platform to players in Nepal. Every guide on this site, from the sign-up walkthrough to the Sugar Rush 1000 rules, is written for someone who wants to know what to expect before spending a rupee: how the account is opened, what a promotion is worth after the terms are read, how a game's mechanics work and how to play from a phone. We publish in Nepali first and in English alongside it.

How the Information Is Checked

Figures on this site are taken from primary sources and dated.

  • promotion percentages, dates and conditions come from the promotion cards inside the platform;
  • game facts such as RTP, volatility, maximum win and paytables come from the studios' published specifications;
  • screens and steps are described from the live platform on the day of writing;
  • where a value can change, such as deposit limits or RTP versions, we say so and point to the place inside the account where the current value is shown.

When a promotion is withdrawn or a game is updated, the affected page is corrected. Readers who spot an outdated figure can report it through the form below.

Our Principles

Four rules shape everything published here.

  1. Adults only: the platform and this site are for people aged 18 and over;
  2. No inflated numbers: a bonus is described together with its wagering and validity, never as free money;
  3. Player safety first: warning signs, limits and self-exclusion are explained on the responsible gambling page;
  4. Plain language: guides are written for a first-time player, in Nepali and English.

Contact
