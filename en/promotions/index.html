Bonus Balance vs Real Balance

Understanding the two balances prevents most disappointment. Real money is what the player deposited or won without a bonus; it can be withdrawn at any time subject to verification. Bonus money is credited under conditions and becomes withdrawable only after the wagering requirement is met within the validity period. Bets placed while a bonus is active are limited by the maximum bet in the card, and a withdrawal requested before the wagering is complete usually cancels the remaining bonus.

Claiming Step by Step

The order is the same for every offer and it starts before the money moves.

  1. Log in and open the promotions section;
  2. Read the card of the offer, including the wagering line;
  3. Select or activate the offer if the card requires it;
  4. Deposit a qualifying amount in the cashier;
  5. Confirm that the bonus appears in the balance before playing.
